Home / About ICOF

Organised by students, built for the next generation of clinicians.

ICOF — the International Congress Of (bio)medical Faculties — brings together medical, dental and biomedical students from across the world for a week of lectures, hands-on workshops and shared research, hosted by the Faculty of Medicine in Skopje.

Every session, workshop and social event is planned end to end by an all-student organising committee, working alongside faculty professors and clinicians.

Whether you're coming to present research, practise a clinical skill or simply meet people from other faculties — ICOF is built around students learning from and with each other.
