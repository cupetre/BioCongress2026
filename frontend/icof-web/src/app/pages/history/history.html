The ICOF chronicle

A history of ICOF

Three editions, one growing student tradition — Skopje, North Macedonia
2023
Origins

ICOF began as an idea shared between a handful of medical students who felt their region lacked a congress built specifically for them — one that treated student research as seriously as any academic conference would.

The founding committee, first planning meeting, spring 2023.

The founding symposium was small: a single day, one lecture hall, and a programme built almost entirely through personal invitations. What it lacked in scale it made up for in intent.

“We didn't have a budget. We had a lecture hall and an idea.”
2024–25
Growth
2nd edition opening ceremony, main auditorium.

The 1st and 2nd Student Medical Congresses expanded ICOF from a single symposium into a genuine multi-day event, with international speakers, a formal scientific committee and its first cohort of student ambassadors abroad.

“By the 2nd edition, we'd outgrown our own expectations.”

Partnerships with the Faculty of Medicine and regional hospitals gave the congress a permanent academic home, while a growing ambassador network began recruiting participants from neighbouring countries.

Today
Today & future
“Each committee hands the congress to the next a little stronger than they found it.”

ICOF now runs as a full three-day congress with a dedicated scientific board, an international ambassador network, and a returning base of partners and sponsors.

3rd edition organising committee, summer 2027.

The organisation remains entirely student-run — each committee hands the congress to the next with a growing archive of what worked, and what to try differently. This page is part of that archive.
