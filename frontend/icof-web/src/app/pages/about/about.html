Fun fact — ICOF's first edition in 2023 ran out of a single lecture hall with zero budget. Three editions later, it welcomes 1,200+ students from 48 countries.

ICOF brings together medical, dental and biomedical students from across the world for a week of lectures, hands-on workshops and shared research, hosted by the Faculty of Medicine in Skopje.

Every session, workshop and social event is planned end to end by an all-student organising committee, working alongside faculty professors and clinicians. Whether you're presenting research, practising a clinical skill, or simply meeting people from other faculties, ICOF is built around students learning from and with each other.
